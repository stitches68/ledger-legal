Privacy Policy

How LEDGER handles Discord account information, screenshot evidence, OCR statistics, and service records.

Plain-language summary: LEDGER uses Discord identifiers, screenshot OCR results, contracts, and credits to run the community economy. You can ask for access, corrections, or deletion.

1. Who operates LEDGER

LEDGER is operated for the R.E.A.P.E.R. Divers community. The Operator is responsible for deciding how the Bot processes data. Contact: reaperdivers@gmail.com or the community moderation team through its Discord server.

2. Information we process

Depending on the features you use, LEDGER may process:

  • Discord account and server data: user IDs, display names, avatar URLs or images, server IDs, assigned roles, and linked unit membership.
  • Contract and transaction records: claims, submissions, approvals, amounts of fictional Blacksite Credits, wallet and unit balances, purchase orders, fulfillment and moderation decisions.
  • Images and proof metadata: uploaded mission screenshots, links to review messages or attachments, image fingerprints or hashes, extracted OCR text and statistics, duplicate-screen flags, and related timestamps.
  • Player identification and profiles: in-game names that members associate with Discord accounts, callsigns, specialties, profile text, service records, statistics, and rankings.
  • Mentorship activity: requests for a mentor, participating member and officer IDs, training-session confirmations, approvals, and rewards.
  • Operational logs: command or error information needed to administer and secure the Bot.

Screenshot contents may incidentally show other players’ names or gameplay information, including people who are not members of the Discord server. These players do not automatically receive individual dossiers or credits.

3. Where the information comes from

Information comes from Discord’s API and server roles, from members or moderators who enter details, and from screenshots submitted through Discord. Tesseract OCR processes submitted images locally on the computer or cloud server running LEDGER; the current OCR workflow does not require sending images to an external generative-AI service.

4. Why we use this information

We use information to operate contracts, prevent duplicate or fraudulent claims, award fictional credits, attribute accepted mission statistics to selected players, maintain dossiers and leaderboards, coordinate mentors and unit rosters, manage shops, resolve disputes, maintain security, and troubleshoot failures. Where required by applicable law, we rely on a valid legal basis for these activities, which may include providing the requested service and legitimate interests in community administration and fraud prevention.

5. Who can see the information

Public or member-visible Discord channels may show contract progress, leaderboards, approved payments, unit affiliation, or chosen dossier details. Authorized moderators can access review materials, disputes, payment records, and mentorship administration as needed. Other server members may see content you submit in public channels.

Discord delivers the Bot’s interactions and attachments; Railway hosts the running Bot and its persistent SQLite database; GitHub Pages hosts this policy website. These providers may process data according to their own terms and privacy notices. We do not sell member personal information or intentionally use it for targeted advertising.

6. Storage, retention, and security

LEDGER’s active records are stored in a SQLite database on the server hosting the Bot, normally a persistent Railway volume. Proof images and review messages may remain accessible through Discord or its attachment system. The Bot may store image hashes or related proof references for duplicate checking even when an image is no longer directly available.

Records are retained while reasonably needed to provide the Bot, keep the ledger consistent, resolve disputes, deter repeated abuse, or meet legal obligations. There is currently no guaranteed automatic deletion schedule for every record. Removing the Bot from a server does not necessarily erase historical database or Discord message data automatically. We use reasonable access controls, but cannot promise absolute security.

7. Your choices and deletion requests

You may ask the Operator to show you the information associated with your Discord ID, correct a mistaken player-name link or stat attribution, or delete personal data when appropriate. Contact reaperdivers@gmail.com or the community moderation team. Include your Discord user ID and a description of your request; do not send a password or bot token.

We will review requests within a reasonable period and honor applicable rights. Some transaction IDs, fraud-prevention hashes, or essential ledger audit records may need to remain in reduced or de-identified form to maintain accuracy or meet legal requirements. Messages and media held by Discord may need separate deletion by their poster, a moderator, or Discord itself. Depending on your location, you may have statutory access, correction, erasure, objection, or complaint rights.

8. Age and sensitive information

LEDGER is intended only for people who meet Discord’s applicable age rules and the server’s participation requirements. Please do not submit government identifiers, passwords, financial card details, medical information, or other sensitive real-world information in contract screenshots, profile fields, or mentorship forms.

9. This policy website

The legal website is a static GitHub Pages site. It does not itself require an account or deliberately set analytics or advertising cookies. GitHub or the user’s browser may still process technical request information as part of delivering the pages. Follow GitHub’s own privacy notice for its service.

10. Policy changes and contact

We may update this Privacy Policy when features, hosting, retention practices, or legal requirements change. The revision date at the top will be updated. For questions or requests, contact reaperdivers@gmail.com.

LEDGER is an independent, unofficial project. These documents should be reviewed by the Operator for accuracy before publication and updated when the Bot’s actual data practices change.
